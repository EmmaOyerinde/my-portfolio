Enterprise Leadership & Delivery

My work extends far beyond standard project delivery to ensure the critical infrastructure powering our communities operates flawlessly. I bridge the gap between complex technical execution and high-level business strategy, guiding enterprise IT, GIS, and operational teams through high-stakes deployments.

Whether orchestrating large-scale technology rollouts, aligning executive stakeholders, or untangling legacy system migrations, my focus is entirely on delivering secure, scalable outcomes.

I have spent over a decade in the utility sector managing systems that serve more than a million customers. This background has shown me firsthand how quickly technical debt and operational silos can derail an initiative. Rather than simply rolling out rigid frameworks, I build pragmatic governance structures that actually work in the real world by blending traditional project management with Agile execution. Most recently, I have been integrating AI to cut through administrative friction and automate workflows. This gives our engineering teams the time and space to do what they do best: innovate.
