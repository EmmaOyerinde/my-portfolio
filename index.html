CLARITY BUILDS TRUSTEvidence, methods & protection+

Trace every number

Open Sources & freshness in the lab for the publisher, reporting period, check time, coverage, and original download. An unavailable or missing value is never converted to zero. Saved data stays labeled as saved.

Reliability has a scope

OEB annual scorecards cover Ontario distributors, excluding loss of supply and major events. Additional utilities use individually sourced annual reports or clearly labelled provincial electricity context. Report methods differ and are not merged into one reliability ranking. Detailed geographic footprints preserve the original local polygons; official Ontario service-area geometry is available as a separate layer. The optional OEB layer retains its own published names and geometry.

Projections are conditional

CER pathways are published scenarios, not promises. The source defines the historical cutoff. Sensitivity adjustments are explicitly your assumptions. Statistics Canada’s monthly energy flows use a different definition from CER end-use demand, so the two are never subtracted to invent a deficit.

Communities, accurately

The NRCan inventory provides settlement coordinates and reported power sources, including regional-grid communities. Publication edition, catalogue coverage, and retrieval time are separate. Inventory edition and source coverage stay attached to every profile.

Privacy by restraint

No analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts and scripts are hosted with the site. Reading preferences stay on your device. News and basemap providers receive the requests needed to display their content. Contact opens your mail application.

Built-in safeguards

Restricted script loading, validated source snapshots, safe rendering, and tightly scoped publishing workflows reduce common web risks. This is a read-only portfolio: it does not accept passwords, payments, or contact-form submissions.
